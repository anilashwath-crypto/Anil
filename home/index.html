Expiry reminders

Open the app once a day (or add it to your home screen) — anything expired or expiring within 7 days is announced here and as a phone notification.

Backup & data

Everything is stored only on this phone (offline). Export a backup file now and then, or move data to a new phone with import.

App lock (login)

Protects the app with a password/PIN and — where the phone supports it — face or fingerprint unlock, using the phone's own screen-lock biometrics. This blocks casual access to the app; the data itself stays on this phone.
